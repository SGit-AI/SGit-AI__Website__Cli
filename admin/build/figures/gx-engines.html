The algorithm beats the engine. Generation numbers (git's commit-graph idea) answer the two history questions in 0.1 ms: about 200 to 50,000 times faster than the engines walking the graph.
Commit histories are deep, not wide. 20,000 hops one after another is the case the Cypher engines handle worst: their shortest-path search advances one level per hop.
SQL needs its plan pinned. SQLite's planner chose a scan for the recursive step: 182 s for the folder walk, 14 ms once the join order is forced.
