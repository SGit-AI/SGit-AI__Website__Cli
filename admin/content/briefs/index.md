# Briefs

The documents this site was built from, published in full as they were given, with their status and date. A brief is never edited to look right afterwards: where one turned out to be wrong, the finding is in [the corrections file](/admin/corrections.html).

The research articles are the other kind of brief the first one asks for, "our new generation of briefs": one topic taken as far as it goes, captured here first. They are on [Research](/articles/index.html).
