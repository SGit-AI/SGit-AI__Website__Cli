Corrections to the brief

The brief is published as given. Where it was wrong, unclear or overtaken, the finding goes here, dated, newest first, and the brief stays as it was.

2026-10-20, the second brief, while building v0.2.0

  1. "send.sk.ai" is send.sgit.ai, and its articles guidance is Every site its own articles. "The main official repo" is read as SGit-AI/SGit-AI__CLI, as in the first brief.
  2. The recent research was not on dev. The six documents of 16 and 20 October were on the CLI's working branch claude/charming-babbage-5hzcxq at 5473571, not yet merged; they were published from there, and every page from them names the branch.
  3. "Research documents that should now be articles" were two different things. Five of the six were drafts written as newsroom articles; one was a coverage map. The drafts became articles; the map, and every earlier research document, went to a new research area. Research is not an article gives the reasoning.
  4. Correction 5 below is overtaken. v0.1.0 published 23 research documents as articles. In v0.2.0, 21 of them moved to /research/ and their old addresses send the reader on; the two that were newsroom drafts stayed as articles, converted to the newsroom's format.

2026-10-20, while building v0.1.0

  1. The names in the transcript are transcription errors. "cli.sketo.ai" is cli.sgit.ai (the repository's README says so); "Escrip. AI" and "sk.ai" are sgit.ai; "the Skit" is sgit. Read throughout as such.
  2. "The API website for the CLI" is read as the CLI's documentation, with the HTTP API in it. The brief goes on to describe a documentation site, so the site is that; the HTTP API pages moved here too, because the CLI is a client of that API and nothing else documents it as a whole.
  3. "Move" cannot be finished from this repository. The pages are copied here and marked as moved, but sgit.ai still serves them and does not point here. Until sgit.ai's team adds links and redirects, both copies exist, and the one here is maintained from now on. The request is on What moved.
  4. "The most technical and the most detailed" needed a line drawn. Drawn at: pages about the command-line tool, its keys, its protocol and its operation moved; pages about vaults as a platform (vault apps, _page.json, the browser bridge, publishing) stayed, since they are about the vault rather than the CLI. Twenty-seven pages moved.
  5. "The articles in the CLI repository" were not articles yet. Two are article drafts for the newsroom; the rest are research notes, designs, reviews, measurements and fix write-ups for Dinis. Twenty-three of the October documents, those that take one topic as far as it goes, are published as articles, with a kind that says which of these each is. Replies to other teams and per-commit review responses were left in the CLI repository.
  6. The guidance "at sgit.ai for how to set up these websites" is two pages. Every sgit repository and the vault guidance. The sibling sites (pki.sgit.ai, sgit.ai itself) supplied the pipeline. Where this site does not yet meet the guidance (the review folder, roles and a board, a release script that checks the live site), it is said on the engineering page and in the reality file.
  7. One difference from the sibling sites, chosen on purpose. pki.sgit.ai's pages are hand-written HTML with the chrome injected; here every page is generated from markdown, because most of this site's content arrives as markdown (sgit.ai's twins, the CLI repository's documents, the CLI's own reference) and converting it by hand would be the drift the guidance warns about.
