The second brief, 20 October 2026: articles, and an area for research

Status: accepted; site v0.2.0 was built from it the same day. Written by: Dinis Cruz, as a message in the session that built v0.1. Published: in full and as given, including the transcription's "send.sk.ai", which is send.sgit.ai (the corrections file records it).

The brief, as given

Great work. Now, can you take a look at the articles section of the send.sk.ai, which has guidance for how to create an articles section here on this website? And then can you look at the main official repo for a bunch of recent research documents that we have pushed that there that should now be articles? Because A, I want to read them as articles. B, we need them. We need those kind of research topics to be kind of independent. Or maybe you need an area on this website just for that more advanced research versus generic articles. So, can you figure out the best way to do that?

What the brief asks for, read plainly

  1. Follow send.sgit.ai's guidance for an articles section: Every site its own articles.
  2. Publish the CLI repository's recent research documents, so they can be read as articles.
  3. Keep research topics independent, possibly in an area of their own, apart from general articles.
  4. Decide the best way, and build it.

How v0.2.0 answers it

AskWhere it is
1. The articles guidanceArticles, in the newsroom's format, held to it by the gate
2. The recent researchthe six documents of 16 and 20 October: five article drafts, now articles, and one coverage map, now research
3. Independent research topicsResearch: every document as written, grouped into topics, one page per topic with its question and where it stands
4. The best way, and whyResearch is not an article, and what was built: What shipped in v0.1 and v0.2
